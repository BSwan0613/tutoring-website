About us

Peer tutoring that actually works.

S&B Tutoring was started by two Laurier students who saw a gap — quality academic support that's affordable, relevant, and student-run. Our tutors have aced the courses you're struggling with and know exactly what WLU professors expect.

We're primarily focused on the BBA program and business core courses, but we welcome students from all faculties. If we can help, we will.

🎓
Peer tutors only All tutors are current or recent Laurier students with proven academic records in the courses they teach.
📍
In-person & virtual Meet on campus at the Waterloo location or hop on a video call — whatever works best for you.

The process

Simple from start to session.

01

Reach out

Fill out our contact form or drop us a message on Discord or Instagram. Tell us what course(s) you need help with.

02

Get matched

We'll pair you with a tutor who has excelled in your specific course and fits your schedule.

03

Book & pay

Confirm your session time and pay securely. Rates are straightforward — no hidden fees or commitments.

04

Start learning

Meet your tutor on campus or virtually. Sessions are flexible — one-on-one or small group, your call.
